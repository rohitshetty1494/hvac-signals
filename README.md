# HVAC App Future Signals

Interactive dashboard of signals and trends from secondary research for a residential HVAC (air conditioner / heat pump) app.

**Live page:** https://rohitshetty1494.github.io/hvac-signals/

It shows:
- Which trends to act on (impact vs years to mainstream)
- Adoption shifts (earlier vs latest survey readings)
- AI capability penetration across smart-home and HVAC players
- A Kano model placing 25 app features by category and market delivery
- A regulatory and policy timeline
- A filterable table of all 89 signals with their sources

The page is a single self-contained `index.html` (data and charting library inlined) and works offline.
Priority positions, AI scores and Kano categories are analyst judgements from the cited evidence.
